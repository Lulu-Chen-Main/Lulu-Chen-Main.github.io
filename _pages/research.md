<p class="research-intro">My work connects atmospheric science, satellite remote sensing, environmental health, and environmental justice. I combine observations and models with demographic, transportation, and health data to understand how pollution varies, who is most affected, and how new measurements can support more equitable decisions.</p>

## Research Approach

- Integrating satellite, ground-based, emissions, transportation, demographic, and health datasets
- Characterizing pollution across space and throughout the day
- Evaluating exposure disparities and environmental-health burdens
- Translating new observations into evidence for more effective and equitable mitigation
